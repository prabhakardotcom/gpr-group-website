workspace_premium Group Leadership Spotlight

Visionary Stewardship Guiding Diverse Frontiers

G. Prabhakar Rao, Chairman & Managing Director of GPR Group, is a JNTU Kukatpally alumnus with an M.Tech in Electrical Power Engineering, an MBA and NISM Series V-A certification. With more than 25 years of professional experience, he began his career in academia and progressed from Assistant Professor to HOD and Vice Principal before moving into entrepreneurship. He now leads GPR Group’s organizations across education, international career services, real estate and financial services.
