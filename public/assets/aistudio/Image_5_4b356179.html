Historic Old Hyderabad · Cultural Soul & Trust

The Soul of Hyderabad: Trust, Hospitality & Humility

Decades of living and working in Hyderabad have ingrained the values of Ganga-Jamuni tehzeeb, genuine human warmth, and accessible opportunities into every initiative led by Chairman Rao. Deeply committed to grassroots community welfare in Kharmanghat, Saroornagar, and broader Telangana, he ensures that quality healthcare and vocational education are placed within reach of every aspiring family, regardless of economic background.

HITEC City & Cyberabad · Future-Forward Growth

The Gateway of Ambition: Empowering Hyderabad’s Next Generation

Guided by the Chairman’s forward-looking conviction that Hyderabad stands as one of the world's greatest innovation and service corridors, GPR Group actively prepares local youth for global mobility. Through GPR LacSo Nursing Colleges, LacSo IHM, Go Grow International, and GPR Infra, he builds vital bridges connecting Hyderabadi talent directly to premier international careers and world-class regional civic infrastructure.
