## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative institutional leadership, fiscal integrity, and cross-sector prestige for a diversified global conglomerate spanning healthcare, hospitality, capital investments, infrastructure, and international advisory services. 

The aesthetic is Modern Corporate with an architectural sensibility. It eschews generic tech minimalism in favor of structured, editorial dignity:
- **Atmosphere:** Established, sovereign, enduring, and meticulously organized.
- **Visual Tenets:** Strict geometric alignment, deliberate whitespace, structured surface layering, restrained hairline micro-borders, and disciplined accent placement.
- **Tone:** Confident and measured. Warm gold accents are reserved strictly for moments of distinction, critical signals, metrics, and premium touchpoints, while deep corporate navies anchor foundational architecture.

## Colors

The palette balances monumental deep navies against crisp architectural light surfaces, energized by restrained prestige metals and cool technical slates.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#0A192F`):** Anchors headers, primary brand marks, hero containers, and authoritative actions. Supported by midnight slate (`#0F172A`) for high-contrast typography and deep navigation bands.
- **Secondary Slate Teal (`#0F766E`):** Used for technical substantiation, operational data tags, infrastructure status chips, and focused interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary Regal Gold (`#D97706`):** Reserved for investment yields, high-value status indicators, premier division crests, and key call-to-action highlights. Supported by `#B45309` (hover/border) and `#F59E0B` (accent indicators).
- **Neutral Foundation:** Slate neutral scale ranging from dark slate text (`#1E293B`, `#334155`), cool structural borders (`#E2E8F0`), muted surface backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`), to pure backdrop white (`#FFFFFF`).

### Surface Application
- Layering follows a strict luminance hierarchy: `Base Background (#F8FAFC)` $\rightarrow$ `Raised Surface (#FFFFFF)` $\rightarrow$ `Sub-pane Surface (#F1F5F9)`.
- Never use the tertiary gold for generic backgrounds or extensive fills; it functions exclusively as a focused prestige accent, indicator, or precision border.

## Typography

The pairing combines **Space Grotesk** for display and headline tiers with **Inter** for data density, narrative body text, and institutional labelling.

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides architectural precision, crisp geometry, and an enterprise scale. Headlines feature slight negative tracking to retain density across corporate publications and portfolio overviews.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Guarantees high micro-legibility in financial tables, legal documentation, healthcare compliance specs, and multi-tier matrices.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular figures are enforced across all reporting grids, portfolio returns, and operational counters.
- **Upper-case Eyebrows:** Sector categorizations and status tags leverage `label-sm` or `label-md` set in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.06em`).

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid provides balance and visual order across varied enterprise sector modules.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 32px (`gutter-lg`) gutters, and 64px (`margin-lg`) canvas margins. Max content container bounded at 1360px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px (`gutter`) gutters, 32px (`margin`) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`gutter-sm`) gutters, 16px (`margin-sm`) margins.

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Enterprise portfolio grids must maintain rigid column-spanning symmetry: 4-4-4 for high-level pillars, 6-6 for bilateral executive summaries, and 8-4 for content-to-metadata enterprise dossiers.
- Component internal padding strictly correlates with the `space-*` scale: Cards use `space-lg` (24px) for desktop down to `space-md` (16px) for mobile screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through high-precision structural boundaries, subtle cool-toned multi-stop shadows, and deliberate surface tints rather than dramatic drops.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface. No elevation.
2. **Level 1 (Structured Cards & Grids):** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px boundary of `#E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Cards on Hover, Executive Menus):** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border shift to `#CBD5E1` and an elevated shadow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.03)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Global Sector Flyouts):** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) surrounded by a 1px border of `#CBD5E1` and a deep architectural shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.1), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.04)`.
5. **Prestige Variant (Featured Asset or Tier 1 Card):** Employs an ultra-fine 1px border tinted in `#F59E0B` (or 20% alpha `#D97706`) with an amber ambient rim glow: `0 0 0 1px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15), 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a restrained, softly geometric shape profile (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and badges feature a sharp-yet-softened `0.25rem` (4px) radius.
- Structural cards, dashboards, and modal dialogs use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Large media wrappers, global navigation bars, and sector modules max out at `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Circular rounding (`9999px`) is strictly forbidden for standard UI inputs and buttons; it is permitted only for micro status pips, avatar portraits, and numerical count indicator dots.

## Components

- **Secondary (Institutional):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0A192F`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Hover: Background `#F8FAFC`, border-color `#94A3B8`.
- **Tertiary / Prestige:** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#B45309`. Hover: Background `#B45309`. Reserved for high-value conversions, investment inquiries, and key prospectus downloads.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#0A192F`, hover background `rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.04)`.

### Badges & Corporate Tags
- Small (22px height), uppercase `label-sm` tracking 0.06em, padding 2px 8px, border-radius 4px.
- **Enterprise Status:** Background `#F1F5F9`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#334155`.
- **Prestige / Capital Status:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`, border 1px solid `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`, text `#B45309`.
- **Operational / Healthcare Status:** Background `rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.08)`, border 1px solid `rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.25)`, text `#0F766E`.

### Cards & Division Panels
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`, border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, border-radius: 8px. Padding: 24px desktop, 16px mobile.
- Header: Incorporates sector uppercase eyebrow (`label-sm`), followed by `headline-md` title.
- Interactive Cards: On hover, border transitions to `#94A3B8`, with elevation shifting to Level 2.
- Featured Division Card: Top border accented with a 3px solid strip of `#D97706`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height: 44px. Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Radius: 4px. Text: `#0F172A` (`body-md`).
- Placeholder: `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border 1px solid `#0A192F`, outline 2px solid `rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.15)`, outline-offset 1px.
- Error state: Border 1px solid `#DC2626`, outline 2px solid `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Dimensions: 18px $\times$ 18px. Border: 1.5px solid `#94A3B8`. Radius: 3px (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Selected State: Background `#0A192F`, border `#0A192F`, check icon crisp white.
- Focus: Outline 2px solid `rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.2)`.

### Lists & Ledger Tables
- Table Headers: Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-sm`, text `#475569`, border-bottom 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- Rows: Background `#FFFFFF`, border-bottom 1px solid `#F1F5F9`, row hover `#F8FAFC`. Height: 52px.
- Tabular figures aligned right for monetary values and percentages.

### External Link Indicators & Action Affordances
- All links connecting across subsidiaries or launching investor portals must carry an integrated external link glyph (`14px` height, angled 45-degree arrow).
- The glyph rests 4px to the right of the anchor text, tinting `#64748B` by default and transitioning smoothly to `#0A192F` or `#D97706` on hover alongside the text underline.
